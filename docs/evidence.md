# Evidence and assumptions

Research checked on 2026-10-01. These public pages informed the concept; they are not access to GitKraken's internal systems. Job availability is unconfirmed.

| Evidence | Source | Design implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Product Associate/Manager description includes subscriptions, billing, organization management, notifications, settings, prototyping, and measurement. | [Public job description](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/product-associate-manager-at-gitkraken-4423054241), [second public copy](https://builtin.com/job/product-associate-manager/9600363) | Demonstrate one workflow spanning these responsibilities. |
| GitKraken documents a case where a subscriber still sees the free plan and should select the licensed organization and sign in again. | [Account and license FAQ](https://help.gitkraken.com/gk-dev/gk-dev-faq/) | Explain the cause of blocked access and a relevant next action. It does not establish the frequency or business impact of this problem. |
| Organization membership, approval of invitations, role permissions, available licenses, and seat purchases interact. | [Organization documentation](https://help.gitkraken.com/gk-dev/gk-dev-organization/) | Model user and administrator perspectives together. |
| Subscriptions belong to organizations; adding seats can incur a prorated charge; quotes can show immediate cost and renewal cost. | [Subscription documentation](https://help.gitkraken.com/gk-dev/gk-dev-subscription/) | Make the two different costs clear before approval. Cost previews already exist in GitKraken. |

## Our assumptions

All names, sample prices, time periods, payment results, feature restrictions, simplified roles, and state transitions in the prototype are our demo policies. The app is an independent concept, not an official GitKraken product or an exact reproduction. See `brief.md` for each chosen rule.

## Unknowns

- Frequency and severity of access confusion among actual GitKraken users.
- Whether GitKraken already has equivalent internal explanations or planned improvements.
- User preference for this proposed flow and any effect on support, retention, or conversion.
- Current status of the vacancy.

## Proposed validation

Ask 3–5 consenting participants to explain why Maya is blocked and complete the access workflow without coaching. Record task completion, time, assistance, mistakes, and comments. Compare observations before and after revisions; do not treat a small sample as statistically conclusive. No participant sessions have taken place yet.
