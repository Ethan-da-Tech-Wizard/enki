REVIEWER TOOLS

Explore the whole story.

These controls change fictitious local data. Choosing a persona is a demo tool, not a sign-in system.

1. Choose a perspective

Maya requests access. Eli reviews the cost and approves. Juniper identities demonstrate a separate organization.

2. Set a payment outcome

Applies to new purchase attempts until changed. Assigning an available seat requires no payment.

3. Restart a scenario

Resets all sample requests, payments, notifications, and activity in this local demo. Resets payment outcome to success.

A two-minute walkthrough

  1. Be Maya. Explain the access blocker, then request a seat.
  2. Switch to Eli. Review $6 now and $72 next period. Approve the simulated payment.
  3. Return to Maya. Open the shared workspace and see the confirmation.
  4. Explore recovery. Reset, choose payment failure, and try again. Switch the outcome to success before retrying.
